Simple action, careful processing

From right click to a safe copy.

The planned workflow preserves the original, then reopens and scans the output copy again before saving it.

01

Inspect

Identify the real format from content, then look for supported personal data, credentials, document metadata, and other selected risks.

02

Remove and transform

Handle each selected finding with a method appropriate to the file format. The original file remains unchanged.

03

Reopen and recheck

Open the output in a fresh worker, confirm that it is readable, and scan it again before saving the copy.

Transparency

What to know before you share.

Privacy

App file processing is designed to happen on the device. A documented network-behavior test will be published with the initial release so this can be evaluated under stated conditions.

Pro purchase and activation will process only information required for payment and licensing. This site uses no analytics or advertising cookies.

Detection limits

Automated detection is best-effort. Results depend on the file structure, selected policy, supported format, and supported rules. FilePreflight cannot determine every fact that your organization considers confidential.

Review the output before sharing it. Unsupported formats and outputs that fail verification are not labeled as rechecked copies.

Terms

Free is planned for commercial use. Pro is planned as a one-time USD 29 license for one user on up to three devices. Final terms will be shown before purchase.

FilePreflight assists with preparation; it does not replace your organization's approval process, legal obligations, or security controls.

Refunds and support

Refunds and payment issues will be handled under the payment provider's process and applicable law. This does not limit a purchaser's statutory rights. Product support is available through the contact link below.
